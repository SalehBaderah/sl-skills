# Example course requests

For a file-reading agent, begin with: "Read SKILL.md and use it for this request." For a chat-only agent, paste PROMPT.md first. Then use or adapt a request below.

## English for work

> Help me build a personal English course for workplace conversations and emails. I can read simple text but struggle to speak. I can study 30 minutes a day, four days a week. Teach in English with simple explanations. Help me establish my level, then plan the roadmap with me before creating lessons.

## Visual design

> Create a course to help me design clear social media graphics. I am a beginner and use a free design tool. I have three hours per week. Include composition, typography, color, and practical critique. Ask about my goals and propose a roadmap before generating materials. If you cannot inspect images, tell me what you need to give useful feedback.

## Creative writing

> Help me build a short-story writing course. I already write occasionally, but my dialogue and endings feel weak. I want drafting exercises, examples, and feedback on revisions. I can work for two hours per week. Plan a manageable course around those goals before creating lessons.

## ML engineering and MLOps

> Help me create a practical course in deep learning engineering and MLOps. I know supervised and unsupervised learning, MLPs, basic training, and basic evaluation. I want to build, debug, evaluate, and deploy models with PyTorch, then progress toward LLM fine-tuning. Teach relevant concepts as we use them. Ask about my Python skills, available hardware, time, and budget before planning. Agree on the roadmap with me before creating notebooks or code.

## Advanced learning

> Help me build an advanced course on editing long-form nonfiction. I have three years of writing experience and do not need introductory grammar lessons. Focus on argument structure, evidence, pacing, and revision. Use a sample of my work to identify gaps, then propose the course.

## A course for other learners

> Work with me to create an introductory financial-literacy course for adult learners. It should cover budgeting, saving, borrowing, and comparing everyday financial choices. Make it accessible to people with limited mathematical confidence. Before writing lessons, clarify the audience, local context, course length, and delivery format. Include instructor notes, practice scenarios, and assessment rubrics after we agree on the roadmap.

## Continue a course

> Read the course overview and progress records in courses/[course-slug]/. Summarize where we left off and help me continue the next activity. Do not restart the intake or assume that completed lessons prove mastery.

## Move between agents or sessions

> Give me a concise handoff containing the approved roadmap, materials created, activities completed, demonstrated abilities, unresolved gaps, and next activity. Include only information I need to continue this course with another agent.
